Comprehensive women’s health is about more than treating illness—it is about preventive care, early detection, and personalized support at every stage of life. At Dr. Kamini Rai’s clinic, every consultation is tailored to your age, health history, symptoms, and personal goals so you receive compassionate, evidence-based care with clarity and confidence.

From routine gynecological checkups and menstrual health concerns to contraception counseling, hormonal balance, pelvic discomfort, and lifestyle-related concerns, we focus on the whole picture of your wellbeing. Whether you are seeking preventive screening, guidance on reproductive health, or support through a life stage change, your care is shaped around your needs, comfort, and long-term health.

Complete care for every stage of womanhood

Our comprehensive approach covers regular health checkups, reproductive health assessments, screening recommendations, and ongoing support for concerns such as irregular periods, PCOS, infertility-related evaluation, menopause, and general women’s wellness. We believe informed conversations and preventive care are the foundation of healthy living, and we take the time to listen, explain, and recommend the most appropriate treatment plan for you.
