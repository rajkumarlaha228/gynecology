Treatment process

IVF is a fertility treatment in which eggs are collected, fertilized in the laboratory, and transferred into the uterus to help couples conceive. At our clinic, each IVF cycle is planned after a detailed evaluation of ovulation, hormones, reproductive anatomy, and the couple’s medical history so the treatment is tailored to individual needs.

The IVF journey usually begins with a consultation and fertility workup, followed by ovarian stimulation, egg retrieval, fertilization, embryo transfer, and a waiting period before pregnancy testing. Depending on the patient’s response and treatment plan, the full process may take several weeks to a few months.

Most patients tolerate IVF well. The stimulation phase is generally manageable, and procedures such as egg retrieval and embryo transfer are performed with careful monitoring and comfort-focused care. Some discomfort can occur, but it is usually mild and temporary, and our team ensures clear guidance throughout.

The number of embryos transferred depends on the patient’s age, embryo quality, medical history, and risk factors. Our approach is designed to maximize success while keeping the procedure as safe as possible, with careful individualized decision-making based on the latest clinical guidance.

Extra embryos are carefully discussed with the patient as part of the individualized treatment plan. They may be cryopreserved for future use, depending on quality and clinical recommendations, helping preserve future opportunities while minimizing unnecessary interventions.

Costs & insurance

The cost of IVF depends on the required investigations, medications, procedures, and whether additional services such as embryo freezing or advanced imaging are needed. We provide a transparent treatment estimate during the consultation so you understand the financial plan before starting care.

Coverage varies by insurer and policy terms. In many cases, certain fertility investigations and treatment components may be partially covered, while others may require self-payment. We can guide you on the documentation and process needed for insurance claims.

We aim to keep your treatment plan clear and transparent. Additional costs may arise if extra medications, advanced testing, embryo preservation, or follow-up procedures are required, and these are always discussed before they are recommended.

We understand that fertility treatment is a significant investment, and financial planning is an important part of care. Depending on the treatment plan, we can discuss flexible payment arrangements to make the process more manageable for our patients.

An IVF package typically includes the core diagnostic workup, medications, ultrasound monitoring, procedure fees, and consultation support. The exact inclusions are individualized based on the patient’s needs, and we provide a detailed cost summary before treatment begins.

Eligibility & preservation

IVF may be recommended for women with ovulation disorders, tubal damage, endometriosis, or unexplained infertility, as well as for couples with male-factor concerns. The decision depends on medical history, age, fertility testing, and the overall goal of achieving a healthy pregnancy.

Age is an important factor because ovarian reserve and egg quality naturally decline over time. Our specialists assess fertility potential carefully and discuss the most appropriate treatment strategy based on age, health, and reproductive history.

Before IVF, we usually perform hormone evaluations, ultrasound scans, uterine and tubal assessments, and semen analysis. These tests help identify any underlying issues and create a personalized treatment plan that offers the best chance of success.

Yes, making healthy lifestyle changes before treatment can support fertility and improve outcomes. We often recommend a balanced diet, regular activity, stress reduction, adequate sleep, and avoiding smoking or alcohol as part of a well-rounded preparation plan.

Yes, IVF may be suitable for single women and same-sex couples depending on their medical and reproductive goals. We provide respectful, individualized counseling to explain the options, procedures, and planning needed for a successful outcome.

Aftercare & results

Most patients are advised to take it easy for a short period after embryo transfer, with light activity and normal rest rather than strenuous exercise. We provide individualized guidance on daily routine, medication use, and when to resume regular activity while keeping stress low.

Success varies based on age, diagnosis, embryo quality, and overall reproductive health. Each case is unique, and our team reviews the best available information with you to set realistic expectations while preserving hope and confidence throughout treatment.

Pregnancy testing is usually scheduled about two weeks after embryo transfer, when the body has had time to respond to treatment. We guide patients carefully on the right timing and follow-up plan to avoid unnecessary stress and ensure accurate results.

Some patients experience mild bloating, fatigue, cramping, or tenderness after IVF treatment, but symptoms are usually manageable. We monitor your responses closely and provide clear guidance on what is normal and when it is important to seek further medical advice.

If an IVF cycle does not result in pregnancy, we review the treatment details with empathy and openness. We discuss possible reasons, next steps, and alternative strategies such as additional evaluations, a modified protocol, or other fertility options to support the next plan forward.
