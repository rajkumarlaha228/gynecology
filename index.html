About Dr. Kamini Rai

Professional Qualifications

Education & Expertise

Dr. Kamini Rai is a qualified gynecologist with expertise in women's healthcare. Her medical education and professional training support her commitment to providing quality gynecological care.

01 · Academic Qualification

Medical Education

Completed MBBS with a strong foundation in medical science, patient care, and women's health, followed by MS in Obstetrics & Gynaecology (OBG).

02 · Advanced Specialization

Minimal Access Surgery & Hysteroscopy

Holds FMAS (Fellowship in Minimal Access Surgery), Diploma in Minimal Access Surgery, and Fellowship in Hysteroscopy, with specialized training in advanced gynaecological procedures.

03 · Professional Experience

Women's Healthcare Expertise

Over 3 years of professional experience in Obstetrics and Gynaecology, providing dedicated care in women's health, pregnancy, and gynaecological treatments.
